# Introduction

This guide contains suggestions on how to use your cooldowns effectively, and tips and tricks to play each phase as effectively as possible.

Dark Knight is a very strong tank in Dancing Mad at both main and off-tank, and its ability to move gauge usage (and therefore damage) around for whichever phase you need the most damage is invaluable during progression.

# Gear

Dark Knight BiS for DMU is currently the same as current Best in Slot, i.e. [https://xivgear.app/?page=bis|drk|current](https://xivgear.app/?page=bis|drk|current). 

## Potion Usage

While potion usage may depend on what your group wants to do, a recommended set of potions would at the start or 2m burst of Phase One, during the first burst of Phase Three, at the start of Phase Four, and in Phase Five.

# Phase One: Kefka

The opener for Phase One differs depending on who is taking the first tankbuster. Both openers assume that the Main Tank is taking the first tankbuster with mitigation, and targeted mitigation (like **The Blackest Night** is presumed to go on the main tank. Buffs are usually sent early so that you get a full usage of buffs at the end of Phase Two, and these openers account for that.

If you're potting in Phase One, Gemdraught can be used after **Unmend**, but it's generally better to use it at the 2 min burst unless you're early in prog and want both a Phase One and Phase Two pot.

**Living Shadow** can be used in the rough range of -5 to -3, but going earlier risks missing buffs. -4 aligns it nicely with your GCD on the second use.

**TBN** should be used very slightly later than -2 to catch an extra autoattack in the case of cotank blocks/parries. One easy way to do this is to use **TBN** when the 2 in the countdown reaches its minimum size and immediately hit Unmend after.

**Carve and Spit** is moved up before **Shadowbringer** so that it can be used before running out for Trines in Phase Two.

As always, the exact order of many things in the opener, such as the second **Shadowbringer** charge, **Salt and Darkness**, **Oblation**, and **Edge**s can be moved around a bit.

It's good to try to time **TBN** with second hit of Revolting Ruin. This is a general best practice to even out mitigation between hits with pre-shields and the extra temporary effectiveness of short mits factored in and allows **Excogitation**-style effects to more easily proc.

Based on when your MP regeneration ticks, you might not have MP for an **Edge** after the last **Souleater**. Weave two **Edges** after **Bloodspiller** in that case to catch the very tail end of buffs.

You can do one full combo after the listed opener and press **Dark Missionary** following the **Souleater** after Kefka recenters to cover Mysterious Magic + Wave Cannon and get it back for Gravitas puddle soaks.

# Phase Three: Chaos and Exdeath

At the start of this phase, if you have two charges of **Shadowbringer** (if you didn't need to send both at the end of the last phase), you can use one immediately targeting the boss you're not going to be holding. As a result, your opener can be something like

**Delirium** (before boss spawns) -> **Scarlet Delirium** -> [target the other boss] -> **Shadowbringer** -> [target the other boss] -> continue

Doing this is fairly 'free' if you can hold the **Shadowbringer** from Phase Two, as it means that you'll still get two **Shadowbringer**s in the upcoming pot window.
